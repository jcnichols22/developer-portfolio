About Me

I'm Josh Nichols, systems tinkerer & homelab builder

I'm a healthcare IT professional — I've worked both sides of clinical technology: as an EMT and EKG telemetry technician where I learned how hospitals actually run, and now in enterprise IT support where I keep those systems up. That combination is rare, and it shapes how I approach every problem: I understand the workflows behind the machines, not just the machines themselves.

Currently I'm the sole IT field support owner for 30+ Atrium Health offices across 7 cities — I own the enterprise-level support, the Windows 11 migrations, the hardware lifecycle, and the relationship with every site. It's demanding, and I love it.

When I'm off the clock, I'm building Velaris: a self-hosted homelab on a two-node Proxmox cluster in a 20U rack. Unraid storage, Docker stacks, Tailscale mesh, Omada VLAN segmentation, AdGuard DNS, Wazuh monitoring, and Ansible automation for all of it. I build my own tools too — things like The Armory (hardware inventory) and Wayfinder (field-work tracking) — and I write it all up on The Lab Rack.

What drives me: I believe your data belongs to you. That's why I self-host, why I learn how things work under the hood, and why I want to help people take control of their own technology. If you want to collaborate, talk tech, or just connect — reach out. I'd love to hear from you.
